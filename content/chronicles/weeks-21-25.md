# AI Chronicles Weeks 21-25

## Week 21 - AI Governance Before AI Innovation
Governance should be designed before deployment. Responsible AI is an operational capability.

## Week 22 - Why Enterprise Architecture Matters Again
AI increases the importance of architecture, integration, identity and data flows.

## Week 23 - Building an AI Centre of Excellence Without Creating Bureaucracy
A successful AI CoE should enable delivery, not become a gatekeeper.

## Week 24 - The First Time an AI Agent Joins a Steering Committee
AI can improve executive decision support while humans remain accountable.

## Week 25 - Measuring AI ROI Beyond Cost Savings
AI value includes productivity, quality, risk reduction and strategic agility.
